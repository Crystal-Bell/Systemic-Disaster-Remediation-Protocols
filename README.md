Systemic Disaster Remediation Protocols
 * Predictive Asset Positioning: Utilizing real-time telemetry and environmental forecasting to pre-position modular gear, thermal reinforcement units, and logistical assets prior to critical stress events.
 * Active Infrastructure Failover: Shifting from static post-disaster recovery to active distributed architectures that dynamically reroute data, power, and supply lines across edge nodes.
 * Autonomous Field Diagnostics: Integrating wearable sensors, remote monitoring, and automated tools to assess structural stability, environmental toxicity, and site damage safely without risking personnel.
 * Decentralized Resource Orchestration: Deploying peer-to-peer mobilization matrices to match localized physical and digital needs instantly, bypassing bureaucratic bottlenecks during active crises.
